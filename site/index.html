How an engagement works

From first call to a program that runs on its own.

  1. Consultation

    A conversation about the problem in front of you, the regulations that apply, and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. Assessment

    Review of current policies, vendors, and case history to find the gaps and the real risks.

  3. Implementation

    Written protocols, physician oversight, and training so your team knows what to do and why.

  4. Ongoing support

    A physician on call for the hard cases, plus updates as the rules change.

About Us

A physician who has worked every side of the problem.

Dr. Johnson is a board-certified Occupational and Environmental Medicine physician who brings a wealth of experience and a unique background to the field of workplace health. Her training began with obtaining her undergraduate degree at Johns Hopkins University followed by a three-year service commitment with Teach For America in Chicago Public Schools. After completing her teaching commitment in 2009, she attended the University of Illinois at Chicago where she obtained her medical degree.

Dr. Johnson went on to complete two years of general surgery residency where she learned to manage a wide range of patients from those needing simple outpatient procedures to those requiring complex care in the surgical intensive care unit. She then obtained certification as a Medical Review Officer (MRO) in 2015 and began working in the Occupational Medicine division for First Advantage, a national employment screening firm. Her work as an MRO exposed her to the world of occupational medicine and the many complex medical issues that can arise in the workplace. To further her training, Dr. Johnson moved to the Bay Area in 2018 and matriculated at the University of California at San Francisco where she completed her residency in Occupational and Environmental Medicine.

As her residency was concluding, the COVID-19 pandemic was just beginning. For her first job after residency, she worked as an Associate Medical Director for WorkCare where she managed COVID-19 compliance for several large clients including multiple TV and film studios. Her role of advising entertainment clients was a natural fit as Dr. Johnson has a long-standing interest in creative work and ran two film festivals while in college. During the pandemic, she developed skills in managing infectious diseases; implementing policies to ensure medicolegal compliance; and communicating with executives, employees, and government agencies alike. She also had the rare opportunity to learn about the unique challenges facing entertainment workplaces. In addition to working with entertainment clients, Dr. Johnson advised companies in a variety of other industries including power, mining, and manufacturing. She also continued her work as an MRO managing drug screening programs for a host of clients.

After spending four years at WorkCare, Dr. Johnson transitioned to her current role as founder and Chief Executive Officer of Polaris Medical Consulting, an occupational medicine firm where she utilizes her unique expertise to provide consulting and clinical services across multiple industries.
